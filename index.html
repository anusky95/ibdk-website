Our Mission

WHY WE
EXIST

Breast cancer is now the most common cancer among Indian women. India's 5-year survival rate lingers around 65%—plummeting below 50% in rural areas. Over 60% of cases are detected at Stage 3 or 4. IBDK was founded to change this reality.

Our Research

RESEARCH
DRIVEN.
PEOPLE
FOCUSED.

At the core of IBDK's model is a guiding principle: no system can be transformed until it is fully understood in all its dimensions.

We began by interrogating the system itself — tracing how women engage with it and where those engagements collapse. This rigorous inquiry exposed, with empirical clarity, the critical voids that continue to undermine breast cancer care in India.

01
SOP BREAST 1 STUDY
Study on the presence and use of standardised treatment protocols.
Finding: SOPs are often missing, even in medical colleges. MDT-based care is rarely implemented.
02
SOP BREAST 2 STUDY
Study of actual Multidisciplinary Team (MDT) practice.
Finding: In 67 of 92 cases reviewed, no MDT discussion took place. Treatment decisions were made without collective input.
03
NAVIGATION STUDY
Explored why many patients discontinue treatment midway.
Finding: Lack of guidance, fear, and system complexity contribute more than cost alone.
04
TREATMENT COMPLETION
Investigated barriers to finishing prescribed treatments.
Finding: Poor completion linked to inaccessibility, high cost, and adherence to non-modern practitioners.
05
SURGICAL TECHNIQUE
Developed a cavity-sparing approach to reduce margin positivity in breast-conserving surgery.
Finding: Safe, effective, and cost-saving alternative.
+
MORE AHEAD

Patient & Practitioner Tools

TOOLS THAT
EMPOWER

IBDK develops simple, evidence-based tools that fit real-world Indian conditions.

Diagnostic Tool
KOLKATA MASTALGIA CHART
A validated diagnostic tool that helps triage patients with breast pain, reducing unnecessary imaging and healthcare burden.
Patient App
ME APP
Promotes regular self-examination through alert-based reminders, step-by-step guidance, and symptom tracking for early detection.
Digital Companion
CHEMOCARE APP
Helps patients track chemotherapy side effects, monitor symptoms, and stay adherent to treatment schedules.
Surgical Framework
DAS'S CLASSIFICATION
An anatomical framework developed by IBDK to guide safe axillary surgery and improve surgical outcomes across settings.
Virtual Platform
MOLECULAR MDT
A virtual multidisciplinary platform integrating molecular profiling into treatment decisions, making personalised care possible outside large hospitals.
Prosthetics
PROJECT SAMPURNA
A soft, low-cost external mastectomy pad designed by breast cancer survivors. Sold with minimal margin, boosting comfort, self-confidence, and dignity after treatment.
Flagship Innovation

BCRADS

Breast Clinical Reporting and Data System — a clinician-led innovation standardising breast cancer assessment in low-resource, high-burden settings.

SCORING SYSTEM

Clinical findings assessed using defined parameters — age, family history, prior radiation, breast asymmetry, skin or nipple changes, lump characteristics, and nodal findings — produce a clear clinical score.

Category Score Interpretation
10–2Normal
23–4Benign
35–6Probably Benign
47–9Suspicious
5≥10Highly Suggestive of Malignancy
🏆

VANESSA MOSS AWARD — LGCW 2025

Received at London Global Cancer Week 2025 for clinical relevance and impact. Adopted across diverse settings including Nigeria, Peru, and other countries.

WHY BCRADS MATTERS

01

Standardises Clinical Breast Examination

Consistent, reproducible scoring eliminates variability across settings and practitioners.

02

Reduces Subjectivity in Assessment

Defined parameters replace intuition with a structured, evidence-based clinical framework.

03

Enables Early Risk Stratification Without Imaging

Critical for resource-constrained settings where imaging access is limited or absent.

04

Supports Timely Referral & Continuity of Care

Each category links to a specific clinical action, from routine follow-up to fast-track referral.
