03 — Content
The 7 Content Pillars

Every Post Lives
Inside One of These.

These 7 pillars ensure you never run out of content ideas. Rotate across them weekly. Each pillar uses your existing assets — doctors, patients, food experts, celebrities, events.

01

🩺 Doctor Wisdom — "Ask the Expert"

Short, punchy doctor videos answering ONE question per post. "What's the #1 symptom women ignore?" "Does a family history guarantee cancer?" Use your oncologists, surgeons, radiologists. 30–90 second format. These go viral because people desperately want credible health info from real doctors.

02

🌟 Survivor Stories — "She Beat It"

Real patient testimonials, video or written. First-person journeys from fear to survival. These are the most emotional, most shareable, most viral content you can create. Film in Hindi, Bengali, English. Keep raw and real — no over-produced feel. Stage 4 survivors are especially powerful. Always end with HOPE.

03

🥗 Food & Prevention — "Eat This, Not That"

Your food experts are gold for the algorithm. Food content gets massive organic reach. "5 foods that reduce your breast cancer risk." "The truth about soy and cancer." "Why turmeric is/isn't enough." Debunking food myths + giving real dietary guidance. Tie to Indian food culture — dal, haldi, ghee.

04

🎤 Celebrity Voices — "They're Talking About It"

Prof. Rangina Hamidi content, any celebrity advocates, conference keynote clips. Behind-the-scenes at events with known personalities. Reaction clips from celebrities watching patient stories. Celebrity endorsement of screening drives. A single celebrity Reel can deliver millions of views and thousands of new followers overnight.

05

📊 Stats That Shock — "Did You Know?"

Data-driven posts designed to stop thumbs mid-scroll. "Breast cancer kills 87,000 Indian women every year. That's one every 6 minutes." Animated carousel facts, bold-text Reels, motion graphic statistics. Prof. Bhramar Mukherjee's Yale research is quotable gold. These get shared by journalists, policymakers, and NGOs.

06

🏥 Behind the Mission — "How We Do It"

IBDK's research, events, team, camps, conferences. "What happens at a free screening camp?" "A day with our breast surgeon." Documentary-style content that builds institutional trust. Donors and partners respond most strongly to this pillar. Show the machines, the labs, the people, the process.

07

🙋 Community Action — "Join the Army"

Challenges, fundraising drives, volunteer stories, "tag your mom and ask her when she last got checked." User-generated content campaigns. Pink ribbon photo challenges. WhatsApp share campaigns. The Pink Army identity — make followers feel they're part of a movement, not just spectators.

04 — Programming
7 Signature Content Series

Your Recurring Shows.
Audiences Come Back For These.

Named content series build habitual viewership. People come back for episodes. The algorithm rewards consistent content patterns. Launch 2–3 series first, add more as you scale.

Series 01

"Doctor on Call"

Format: 60–90 second Reel. One doctor. One question. Every Tuesday.

Topics: Signs, myths, screening age, genetic risk, post-surgery life, mental health.

Why it works: Search intent — millions Google "breast cancer signs in Hindi" monthly. Your videos rank.

Series 02

"Pink Conversations"

Format: 20–45 min podcast/YouTube interview. Monthly.

Guests: Survivors, oncologists, policy makers, celebrities, food experts, global researchers.

Why it works: Authority-building. Clip into 10+ Reels per episode. One shoot = 2 weeks of content.

Series 03

"She Survived"

Format: 3–5 min short documentary. Bi-weekly on YouTube + condensed Reel.

Structure: Diagnosis day → darkest moment → treatment → life now.

Why it works: Highest shareability. Women tag their mothers, sisters, friends. Organic reach explosion.

Series 04

"Food vs Cancer"

Format: 45–60 sec Reel. Food expert on camera with actual food. Weekly.

Topics: Turmeric, soy, alcohol, processed foods, Indian diet tips, anti-inflammatory foods.

Why it works: Food content gets 3–5x higher organic reach on Instagram. Prevention angle attracts healthy audience too.

Series 05

"Myth Busted"

Format: Doctor on camera, one myth, 30–45 seconds. "MYTH: Only older women get breast cancer. FACT:..."

Why it works: Controversy + correction = shares + saves + comments. Algorithm loves all three. Debate in comments = more reach.

Series 06

"Pink Army Spotlight"

Format: Monthly UGC feature. Highlight a community member — volunteer, donor, survivor, camp organizer.

Why it works: Featured people share to their entire network. Community feels seen. Builds loyalty engine. Free viral distribution.

Series 07

"Research in 60 Seconds"

Format: Take a real finding from IBDK's journal or global research. Explain it for a non-medical audience in 60 seconds. Monthly.

Why it works: Shared by doctors, researchers, LinkedIn. Positions IBDK as a thought leader, not just a charity.

06 — Copywriting
Hook Formulas That Go Viral

The First 2 Seconds
Decide Everything.

If the hook fails, the video dies — regardless of how good the rest is. The algorithm decides in the first 2–3 seconds whether to push your content. Use these proven templates and adapt them to IBDK's topics.

H01
"Most doctors won't tell you this…" Use before a counterintuitive medical fact. Creates authority gap + curiosity. Example: "Most doctors won't tell you this about mammograms and dense breasts."
H02
"I was diagnosed at 28. Here's what saved my life." First-person survivor hook. Age specificity creates shock. Personal stakes immediately established. Every woman watching thinks of herself.
H03
"Stop ignoring THIS. Your body is warning you." Action-imperative + mystery. Works for symptom awareness content. Strong urgency without being fear-mongering.
H04
"Every 6 minutes, an Indian woman dies from breast cancer. We can change this." Statistic + call to action structure. The statistic stops the scroll; the CTA redirects to hope. Perfect for awareness campaigns.
H05
"We tested 1,000 women. The results shocked us." (for conference/research content) Research hook format. Numbers + surprise = irresistible. Works especially well on YouTube and LinkedIn for professional audiences.
H06
"MYTH: [common belief]. TRUTH: [the real answer]." The myth-busting format. Direct on-screen text overlay. Works for food myths, age myths, self-exam myths. Gets shares from people who believed the myth and want to warn others.
H07
"Tag your mom. She needs to watch this." Direct CTA-as-hook. Forces immediate social action. Women sharing with mothers = exponential organic reach into 40–60 age demographic who wouldn't otherwise see your content.
H08
"I thought I was fine. I was wrong." (survivor opening line) Creates immediate identification and dread. Everyone has had this thought. The cognitive dissonance drives watch-through completion. Completion rate signals algorithm to push wider.

💡 Minimum Equipment for Maximum Quality

You don't need expensive equipment to go viral — you need good AUDIO and good LIGHTING. Get 3 lapel mics (₹2,000–5,000 each), 2 ring lights (₹3,000 each), and use iPhone or mid-range Android in 4K mode. A ₹15,000 setup beats a ₹2,00,000 studio if the content is real and the story is authentic. Doctor filming a Reel in a hospital hallway is MORE authentic than a polished studio set.

📊 The 3 Metrics That Actually Matter

1. SAVES on Instagram — More predictive of algorithm boost than likes. Target: 3–5% save rate.
2. Watch-through rate on YouTube — Get above 50% and YouTube pushes your video. Improve by front-loading value.
3. Share rate — If people share your content to their stories or DMs, you're in the algorithm's favorite zone. Target: every "She Survived" video should be shareable enough to be sent to a family member.

🔥 The Fastest Paths to 1M Views for IBDK

Path 1 — Celebrity activation: Prof. Rangina Hamidi or any Bollywood actor posts a Reel tagging IBDK. One share = potential millions.

Path 2 — Survivor story: A stage 4 diagnosis story told in first person with raw emotion and a hopeful end. These get shared by millions of women tagging their loved ones.

Path 3 — Shocking statistic Reel: "1 Indian woman dies from breast cancer every 6 minutes" in bold text over emotional music. Simple. Devastating. Shareable.

Path 4 — #PinkArmyChallenge in October: Challenge people to film themselves in pink and tag 3 women to get checked. Low barrier. High identity value. October amplification guaranteed.
